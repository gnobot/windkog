The KOG System

The core of our technology is the patented Conical Helicoid Wind Turbine, a high-efficiency apparatus designed for superior performance in a variety of wind conditions.

Complementing the turbine is a Continuously Variable Transmission (CVT) that ensures the generator operates at its optimal rotational speed, maximizing energy capture from every gust of wind.
